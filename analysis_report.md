## 1. Channel Performance and Listing Audit

### 🟢 Booking.com Listing Audit
* **Current Status:** Live, but extremely underperforming.
* **Rating:** **9.5 / 10** (Exceptional) – However, this is based on only **4 reviews**.
* **Major Brand/SEO Error:** The URL slug is misspelled as:
  `https://www.booking.com/hotel/th/modernfes-poor-villa-khao-yai.html`
  The word **"pool"** is misspelled as **"poor"** in the URL. This severely harms search engine optimization (SEO) and search visibility within Booking.com, while also looking highly unprofessional to users.

## 4. Why the OTA Booking Rate is Only 10% (Diagnostic)

1. **The "Poor Villa" Slug Disaster (Booking.com):**
   Having the URL slug written as `modernfes-poor-villa-khao-yai` destroys SEO indexation for "Pool Villa" and looks unprofessional. It signals poor listing quality control.
2. **Extreme Social Media Bias:**
   The property focuses all marketing on Facebook and Line. While this saves them the 15-20% OTA commission, it limits their market to domestic travelers who follow Thai Facebook groups, completely missing high-spending international tourists and corporate bookings on Booking.com/Agoda.
3. **Severe Lack of Social Proof (Reviews) on OTAs:**
   Having only 4 reviews on Booking.com and 21 on Agoda over nearly 2 years makes the listing look inactive or untrustworthy compared to competitors with hundreds of reviews.
4. **Uncompetitive and Static OTA Pricing:**
   OTAs feature high baseline rates (฿9,500+) with standard templates, whereas direct booking deals on Facebook are more dynamic and cheaper. There is no price parity or custom direct-booking incentives loaded onto OTAs.
5. **Confusing Agoda Inventory Setup:**
   Listing both a "Deluxe" and "Grand" pool villa for a single-unit property confuses guests and creates calendar syncing issues.
